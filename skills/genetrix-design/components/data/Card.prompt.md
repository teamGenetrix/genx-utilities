**Card** — surface container. `dark` for noir cover/stat panels, `accentTop` for the red top rule (pillars, voice), `interactive` to lift on hover.

```jsx
<Card accentTop><h4>Stratégie et planification</h4><p>Cap clair, priorités posées.</p></Card>
<Card dark><StatFigure value="+98%" label="de satisfaction" onDark /></Card>
```
